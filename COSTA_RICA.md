## The situation in Costa Rica

Why a personal-finance simulator, and why here. No knowledge of the country is needed: amounts also come
in dollars, and local words are explained below. Consulted on 2 November 2026.

### In one minute

- **Debt takes a third of the paycheck.** On average, a third of monthly income goes to paying debts. For
  people who earn under $1 000 a month, it is more than half.
- **Almost nobody was taught how to handle it.** Eight out of ten people surveyed never had any financial
  education.
- **The traps are not theoretical.** Investigators counted 2 930 cases of street lenders in 2025, with
  interest from 20% to 100%, and the loan offers arrive by WhatsApp, Facebook and TikTok.
- **So the game uses the real rules.** You live on the real minimum wage, with the real deductions and a
  card under the real legal cap, and you practice before it costs you anything.

### 1. Debt is ordinary

A survey of 1 200 adults aged 18 to 70, in July 2025, with a margin of error of 2.8%. All from the
[OCF, reported by Delfino.cr](https://delfino.cr/2025/10/aunque-los-costarricenses-comprometen-en-promedio-menos-dinero-para-el-pago-de-deudas-el-nivel-de-endeudamiento-es-alto-para-buena-parte-de-la-poblacion).

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **34%** | Share of monthly income that goes to debts, on average. It was 38% in 2023 and 52% in 2020 |
| **58.3%** | The same share for people earning under ₡500 000 (about $1 000) a month |
| **14.7%** | The same share at ages 18 to 24, when debt has not piled up yet: the moment to practice |
| **87%** | Adults with some debt, of any size. It sounds worse than it is, so the 34% says more |
| **1 in 3** | Households with months when money arrives late and they have to borrow |

### 4. What the game copies from reality

| In the game | The real rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross salary of ₡373 092 (about $750) | The 2026 minimum wage of an unskilled worker | Decree 45303-MTSS, in [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/costa-rica/salario-minimo-2026-en-costa-rica-nuevos-montos-para-el-sector-privado/) |
| 10.83% off the salary | Worker's social charges in 2026: 5.50 + 4.33 + 1.00 | [Our review of the engine](docs/ANALISIS_RUBRICA_README_MOTOR.md) |
| No income tax up to ₡918 000 | 2026 brackets: 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% above that | Decree 45333-H, same review |
| Aguinaldo of one twelfth | Free of charges and of income tax | Law 2412, same review |
| Card at 3% a month | 36% a year, under the cap of 36.48% | BCCR, in the table above |
| Lender at 20% a month | The lowest rate the OIJ found | OIJ, in the table above |

We simplified two things: income tax has no credits for children or a spouse, and the prices of the town
are examples, as the game itself says.

### 5. What the player practices

| What happens in Costa Rica | What the player does |
| --- | --- |
| A third of income goes to debts | Lives month by month on the real salary |
| Installments with costs nobody mentions | Asks the seller for the total, the insurance and the commission, out loud |
| Paying only the minimum | Sees the balance grow at the real rate |
| A lender one message away | Asks what it costs, and meets the collectors if they sign |
| A friend with a sure business | Asks where the money comes from before putting theirs in |

### How far to trust these numbers

- **We read most of them in the press.** National media quoting the institution, not the original reports.
  Each row links to where we read it.
- **Two counts of gota a gota do not match.** The OIJ gave 2 930 cases for 2025 at a press conference;
  another of its counts, in [Teletica](https://www.teletica.com/nacional/prestamos-gota-a-gota-acumulan-mas-de-1600-denuncias-en-menos-de-dos-anos_415829),
  gives 1 662 complaints for 2025 and 2026 together. They probably count different things. Neither is a
  number of victims.
- **The survey and the police point in different directions.** In the survey, people who took a gota a gota
  fell from 12% to 3% (about 92 000 people in three years), while investigators report more cases. One asks
  people, the other counts reports, so we do not add them up.
- **We cannot claim yet that the game works.** There are no pilot results to publish, so this page describes
  the problem and the design, not an effect. Class Mode and the telemetry are there to measure it, and the
  results will go in `docs/pilot/`.
